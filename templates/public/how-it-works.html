How working with us actually works.

No accounts, no subscriptions, no checkout. Here's each service and the exact steps behind it.

Sourcing

Tell us what you need. We find it, verify it, and bring back real options.

  1. Submit your product, spec, quantity, and timeline.
  2. We identify and contact potential suppliers.
  3. We verify registration, certifications, and samples.
  4. You review vetted options and decide how to proceed.
Submit a sourcing request

Consultation & meetings

Not ready to submit a full request? Talk to us directly first.

  1. Tell us about your business and what you're trying to do.
  2. Propose a time, or we'll reach out to schedule one.
  3. We meet, review your situation, and advise on the right approach.
  4. You decide whether to move forward with a request.
Book a consultation Propose a meeting time

Supply submissions

Have inventory to move? Submit it for review, not a public listing.

  1. Submit product, quantity, origin, and certifications.
  2. We verify your registration, license, and samples.
  3. We check pricing against current market rates.
  4. Once cleared, we present it directly to buyers. Nothing is published publicly.
