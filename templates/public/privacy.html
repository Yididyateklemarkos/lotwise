Privacy policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. What we collect

When you submit a form on this Site, we collect the information you enter: your name, company, email, phone, country, and any details or photos you include.

2. How we use it

  • To follow up with you directly by email or phone
  • To review and run due diligence on sourcing requests and supply submissions
  • To keep an internal record of who we've spoken with and what was discussed

3. What we don't do

We do not sell your personal data. Supply submissions are reviewed internally and never published or shown to other visitors. There is no public marketplace or listings board.

4. Who sees it

Submissions go to us directly. We may share relevant details with a specific counterparty only where doing so is necessary to advance a deal you've asked us to help with, and only after discussing it with you.

5. Data retention

We retain submitted information for as long as it's useful to our working relationship, and for a reasonable period after to keep records of past engagements.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information you've submitted at any time via the contact form.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted storage of any login credentials, to protect data submitted through the Site.

8. Cookies

The Site uses only the minimal session cookie required for our own admin login. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact form.
