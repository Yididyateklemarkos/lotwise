A closed registry for people who actually move volume.

Lotwise exists because container-scale trade runs on trust, and trust is expensive to build from a cold inbox. We built a registry where every member — supplier or buyer — has already been checked out, so the conversation can start where it should: with the deal itself.

Every listing, every closed deal, every returning member adds to a track record that raises the bar for everyone in the registry.
