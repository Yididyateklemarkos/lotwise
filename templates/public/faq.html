Frequently asked questions

Does Lotwise take a commission?

No. Membership is a flat monthly subscription. Whatever you close with another member is entirely yours.

How does verification work?

You submit your company details and trade documents after signing up. Our team reviews them by hand before your account is approved.

When do I pay?

Only after your application is approved. Once approved, you choose a plan to activate full access to the registry.

What's the minimum volume to list or source?

One full container (FCL). Lotwise is built for container-scale bulk trade, not small parcels.

How does contact happen after a match?

Once a buyer inquires on a listing, a connection thread opens and both parties' WhatsApp or Telegram contact is shared so you can take the conversation further directly.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. There's no lock-in — cancel your plan whenever you like.
