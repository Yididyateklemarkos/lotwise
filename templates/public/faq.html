Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to work with you?

No. There's no login or dashboard. You submit a request — credentials, sourcing, consultation, meeting, or supply — and I follow up directly.

Do you take payment on the site?

Never. No payment of any kind is processed here. Commercial terms are agreed directly between the parties I'm representing.

How does the credentials request work?

Submit the form and I'll send my business registration, references, and track record straight to your email — no cost, no obligation to move forward.

I have supply to sell — will it be listed publicly?

No. Supply submissions are never published. I review the details privately, run due diligence, and only then start presenting the opportunity to qualified buyers.

What's the minimum volume you work with?

Generally container scale (FCL) bulk trade, not small parcels — but submit a sourcing request and I'll tell you honestly if it's a fit.

How fast do you respond?

I review every submission by hand, usually within a day or two.
